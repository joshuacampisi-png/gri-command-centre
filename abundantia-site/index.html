Our Story

Born of the forest, made for the ritual

Abundantia began with a simple belief — that what we put on our skin should be as honest as what grows in the wild. We forage for the purest botanicals and cold-press them into a single, uncompromising elixir.

No fillers. No synthetic fragrance. No shortcuts. Every 60ml bottle is blended, decanted and sealed by hand in small batches, then nestled in our signature amber glass to protect the oils from light.

The result is a grooming ritual that feels less like routine and more like reverence — a daily moment to slow down and tend to yourself.

The Botanicals

Four wild ingredients, nothing else

Every drop is built on a foundation of organic, ethically sourced botanicals — chosen for what they do, not for how they sound on a label.

01

Camomile

Matricaria chamomilla

A gentle, golden flower extract prized for calming inflammation. Camomile soothes redness and quiets reactive, sensitive skin — making the elixir safe for daily use, even beneath a fresh shave.

For Sensitive Skin
02

Castor Oil

Ricinus communis

Cold-pressed and unrefined, our castor oil is rich in ricinoleic acid to lock in moisture and create the ideal environment for fuller, healthier beard and scalp growth over time.

Strengthens & Hydrates
03

Wild Rosemary

Salvia rosmarinus

Hand-harvested from coastal hills, wild rosemary invigorates the scalp and awakens the senses with a clean, herbaceous note. A traditional botanical long associated with vitality and shine.

Stimulates Follicles
04

Golden Jojoba

Simmondsia chinensis

The closest botanical match to skin's own sebum, jojoba balances oil production so the elixir absorbs in seconds — deeply conditioning, yet weightless and never greasy.

Lightweight Finish
