Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Placeholder — to be reviewed against the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and APPs before launch.

Peptide Tests Australia ("PTA", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. Information we collect

  • Booking details: your name, email address, and the details of the sample(s) you submit for testing.
  • Optional details: any notes, peptide descriptions or batch numbers you choose to provide.
  • Payment information: processed by our third-party payment provider; we do not store full card details. Payment provider to be confirmed.
  • Technical data: basic website analytics such as device and page interactions. Analytics tooling to be confirmed.

2. Why we collect it

  • To process your booking, arrange laboratory testing and deliver your Certificate of Analysis.
  • To communicate with you about your test, including confirmations and results.
  • To improve our service and meet our legal and record-keeping obligations.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary with our verified third-party, TGA-approved partner laboratory to perform your test, and with service providers such as our payment processor and email provider. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law.

4. Confidentiality of results

Your test results are treated as confidential and are provided to you. We do not publish individual results or link them to your identity without your consent, except where required by law.

5. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. Information may be stored using reputable cloud infrastructure. Hosting and data-location details to be confirmed.

6. Retention

We retain booking and result records for as long as necessary to provide the service and meet legal obligations, after which they are securely deleted or de-identified.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you. To make a request or raise a privacy concern, contact us via the booking form on our home page. Privacy contact email to be inserted.

8. Cookies

Our site may use essential cookies and basic analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Cookie banner/consent tooling to be confirmed if required.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.

10. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your personal information, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.
