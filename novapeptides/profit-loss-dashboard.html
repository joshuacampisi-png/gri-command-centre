How it's worked out

Profit = your retail price − cost. Cost is the landed cost per vial (both China orders pooled, freight shared across all products), converted to AUD at the rate up top.
  • Vial only cost = the bare landed cost of that peptide vial.
  • Full kit cost = vial + kit extras (BAC water, syringe, swabs, packaging). Default A$6 — set it to what your kit supplies actually cost so the kit profits are exact.
  • Margins are huge (~80%+) because the China landed cost is very low against AU retail — double-check the kit-extras figure is realistic.
  • Reta RT30 and standalone BAC water aren't listed here (no retail price given) — tell me their prices and I'll add them.
