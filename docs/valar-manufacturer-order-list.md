## Notes (internal — do not send)

Ranking logic, so it can be defended or changed:

- Retatrutide is the revenue pillar. RT20 leads Tier 1 as the mid-dose most buyers
  land on; RT30 and RT10 sit in Tier 2 because they are proven but flank the middle.
- KLOW at number 2 — highest-value blend in the range and already selling.
- PDA at number 3 is the one aggressive call. It is the stable BPC-157 analogue and
  the fastest-growing line in recovery, with a direct upgrade path off BPC-157 and
  Wolverine+, both proven sellers. It has warm buyers waiting, not a cold start.
- Wolverine+ 30mg and 40mg rank above the existing 20mg because the strength ladder
  is the upsell — buyers who liked 20mg trade up.
- Tier 3 nootropics (Semax, Selank, the blends, DSIP) are unproven for us. The blends
  rank above the singles because a blend carries a better margin and a clearer story.
- Thymosin Alpha-1 was already an enquiry-only line on the catalogue page, so demand
  is proven but sales are not. Medium until it converts.
- Tier 4 is genuinely speculative. Snap-8 is cosmetic and sits outside the injectable
  range entirely. TB-500 standalone ranks last because it already sells inside
  Wolverine+, so standalone demand is thin.
