## Notes (internal — do not send)

### Provenance — read before trusting this ranking

NOT derived from Valar sales data. Built from the repo catalogue and stocktake, then
reordered against an August 2026 web and community research pass.

The authoritative source is `nova-sales.json` on the Railway volume — the line-item
sales log written by `server/lib/nova-sales-store.js`, holding product, qty, price,
cost and margin per order. Rank on units sold and margin contribution from that file
before committing to a large order. Ranks 12-17 and 22-24 are all existing products
whose position could be settled with real numbers rather than inference.

The market brief at market-brief-8ptjo8.vercel.app could not be read — blocked by the
session network egress policy. Its contents are NOT reflected here.

### What the research changed, versus the previous draft

- BPC-157 moved 13 -> 12 and into the top of Tier 2. It is the highest-demand research
  peptide by search volume, ahead of everything except the GLP-1s. Previously buried.
- PDA moved 3 -> 11. It shares the identical 15-amino-acid sequence as BPC-157 and
  differs only in salt counterion (arginine vs acetate), with no peer-reviewed study
  as a distinct molecule. It substitutes for BPC-157 rather than adding demand, so it
  should not outrank it. Kept in Tier 1 because the marketing angle is genuinely
  selling, but demoted below every proven line.
- Semax + Selank blends moved 21/22 -> 7/8. Nootropics are one of the fastest-growing
  peptide categories and the Semax + Selank stack is the most common combination in
  that category. The blends outrank the singles (20, 21) because the stack is what
  people actually buy.
- Wolverine+ 30mg and 40mg moved 4/5 -> 18/19. They are untested line extensions of an
  existing product, not independent demand. Ranking them above proven sellers was
  wrong.
- Retatrutide ladder consolidated at 1-3. GLP-1 is the single largest demand pool in
  the market, and Retatrutide is the growth engine of this catalogue.
- CJC-1295 DAC held at 16 and Tesamorelin at 17, both on evidence: CJC-1295 with
  Ipamorelin is the most-used GH stack by volume, and Tesamorelin is FDA-approved with
  trial data on visceral fat.
- GHRP-2 and GHRP-6 stay in Tier 4. They are older GH secretagogues largely superseded
  by the CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin stack.

### Gaps this research exposed

- Tirzepatide and Semaglutide are the two highest-demand peptides in the market and
  are enquiry-only on the catalogue page. Absent from a catalogue-launch order.
- Ipamorelin is missing while CJC-1295 DAC is stocked, yet CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin is
  the most-used GH stack by volume. We are selling half a stack and losing the attach.

All three are now on the brief as a separate quote request rather than ranked, so we
get pricing without committing.

### Caveats

- Search volume is category demand, not Valar demand. A peptide can be large globally
  and dead in an Australian customer base.
- Several sources are vendor SEO pages with a commercial interest in the compounds
  they rank. Directionally useful, not authoritative.
- The 18 NEW lines can never be evidence-based from our own data. They are judgement.
