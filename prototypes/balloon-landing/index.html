Frequently asked

How long do helium number balloons last?

Our 34" foil number balloons stay inflated for 3–5 days indoors when treated gently. Heat and direct sun shorten that significantly.

Will the balloons fit in my car?

The number balloons are roughly 86cm tall. A small hatchback can usually fit one or two with the seats down. Bring a friend if you can.

Can I order on the day?

Yes — same-day orders are accepted between 7:00am and 2:00pm AEST, with at least 1 hour lead time for inflation.

What if I need to cancel or change my order?

Get in touch as soon as possible. We can usually adjust colour or numbers up until inflation starts.
